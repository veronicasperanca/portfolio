Design System

Tokens, Componentes,
Escala

A Design System isn't a component library. It's a shared language that lets distributed teams speak with one voice — visually, structurally, and semantically.

My approach starts with tokens (the atomic decisions of color, spacing, and typography), moves on to componentes documentados e culmina em usage and governance guidelines — ensuring adoption and consistency over time.
