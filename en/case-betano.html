Contexto & Scenario

Betano is one of the largest sports betting and casino gaming platforms in Europe and Latin America, with millions of monthly active users. In 2024, the company identified critical opportunities for improvement in its digital platform — an interface that had grown organically over the years, accumulating visual inconsistencies, fragmented flows, and an information architecture that hadn't kept pace with the growth of its user base.

The project was born from the need to scale the experience without losing the essence that made the platform familiar to existing users — one of the most delicate tasks in product design: evoluir sem alienar.

"The biggest risk wasn't redesigning. It was redesigning without deeply understanding why users stayed — and what made them leave."

O Desafio

The platform faced three central tensions:

  • Carga cognitiva elevada: deep menus with up to 4 navigation levels turned finding a game into an effortful task rather than an enjoyable one.
  • Systemic visual inconsistency: over 40 button variations identified in the initial audit — each team had created its own patterns over time.
  • Fragmented betting flow: the path between discovering and confirming a bet had unnecessary friction at 3 critical points identified through funnel analysis.

Process of Design

1. Research and Diagnosis

The project began with an intensive discovery phase — combining quantitative data with deep qualitative listening:

  • 6-month analysis of behavioral data (Mixpanel + Hotjar) to map drop-off points and friction
  • 12 contextual interviews with users at different engagement stages (new, regular, power users)
  • Comparative benchmarking against 8 industry platforms, evaluating UI patterns against Nielsen's heuristics
  • Full audit of the existing interface — cataloguing 340+ screens and identifying 87 critical inconsistencies

A Solution

Rethinking the Information Architecture

Applying Hick's Law (the more options, the longer the decision takes), we redesigned the navigation from a mega-menu model into a progressive-context architecture: the user only sees what's relevant to the moment — and the platform learns from them over time.

ANTES

DEPOIS
2 levels + smart context

Design System as Foundation

Before redesigning a single screen, we built the foundation: a Design System with 68 documented components, design tokens across 3 levels of abstraction, and usage guidelines. This ensured every visual decision was replicable, auditable, and scalable across teams in 4 countries.

  • 68 components documented with variants, states, and accessibility notes
  • 3-layer token system: primitive, semantic, and domain-specific
  • Figma library with 1,200+ versioned styles and components
  • Accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA) built into the process

Reshaping the Betting Flow

Applying Csikszentmihalyi's Flow Theory, we redesigned the core betting flow to create a state of frictionless engagement: challenge calibrated to the user's skill, immediate feedback, and a sense of control at every step.

Etapa 01 · Acesso & Home

Boas-vindas com contexto imediato

The homepage was redesigned with progressive hierarchy: the content most relevant to the user's profile appears first. No action is required — the system learns and prioritizes automatically.

Hick's Law applied Reduced from 4 to 2 navigation levels — fewer options, faster decisions.
Contexto progressivo Personalized content based on player history, with no manual setup.
Etapa 02 · Descoberta de Jogo

Filtros que reduce noise

The game catalog was reorganized with contextual filters and quick-selection chips. Users now find what they want in fewer than 3 clicks — down from 8 previously.

Filtro progressivo Chips replace nested dropdowns — complexity disappears without losing control.
Search contextual Search field with predictive suggestions based on betting history.
Stage 03 · Bet Selection

Bet slip com feedback em tempo real

The side betting panel was rebuilt with instant feedback. Every selection confirms visually and the user never loses track of how much they're betting.

Feedback imediato Flow Theory: instant visual response to every action keeps the player in a state of engagement.
Bet slip persistente Panel always visible without blocking content — 2-column layout with managed z-index.
Stage 04 · Confirmation

Um clique, zero doubt

The confirmation screen was designed to eliminate anxiety: it summarizes the bet details clearly, with a centered primary action button and an always-accessible edit option.

Clarity before commitment Visual summary of the details eliminates errors and reduces support load — -40% confirmation calls.
Non-destructive editing The user can change any detail without losing the selection already made.
Stage 05 · Feedback & Engagement Loop

A result that invites the next game

The results screen was redesigned to be a re-engagement point. Regardless of outcome, the user gets context, relevant suggestions, and encouragement to continue — with no pressure.

Variable reward loop Inspired by game design: unexpected rewards sustain engagement without manipulation.
Performance context The player immediately sees their history — increasing the sense of progress and control.

Aprendizados & Reflections

Working on platforms with millions of active users teaches you that design isn't about personal preference — it's about data, hypotheses, and responsible iteration. Every UI change, however small it seems, has consequences at scale.

The deepest lesson from this project: a Design System doesn't start with components. It starts with a language decision — and it requires as much semantic care as visual care to last more than 6 months in production.
