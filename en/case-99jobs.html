Contexto & Oportunidade

99Jobs is an HRTech platform specialized in hiring processes for major corporations. In 2022, it identified a unique opportunity: traditional hiring processes were perceived by candidates as passive, anxiety-inducing experiences — lengthy forms, no feedback, and no sense of progress or accomplishment.

At the same time, client companies were suffering alarming drop-out rates during the process — qualified candidates abandoning the funnel before the final stage, representing lost investment and recruiter time.

"What if the hiring process were as engaging as a good game? Not to make it trivial — but to make it human."

O Problema Real

Three core pain points were identified in initial research with candidates and HR teams:

  • Candidatos: the absence of feedback during the process generated anxiety and disengagement — "it feels like I'm talking into a void"
  • Recrutadores: long forms discouraged qualified candidates who were introverted or less familiar with the traditional format
  • Empresas: data collected by the process didn't reflect real work behaviors — hard skills were assessed, soft skills stayed invisible

O Framework of Gamification

The project was anchored in two complementary frameworks: Octalysis Framework by Yu-Kai Chou (which maps the 8 core drivers of human motivation) and Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan), which differentiates extrinsic from intrinsic motivation.

The premise was clear: superficial gamification (points and badges with no purpose) would be counterproductive. What was needed was meaning, not just stimulus.

A Solution of Design

Journey as Narrative

We redesigned the hiring process as a narrative told in chapters: each stage has a name, a communicated purpose, and a moment of recognition. The candidate doesn't "take a test" — they "complete the values-discovery mission" or "present their perspective in the creative challenge."

Feedback em Tempo Real

We created a micro-feedback system that accompanies the candidate: completion indicators, recognition animations when finishing stages, and contextual messages that humanize the process. Candidates always know where they stand, what's next, and that they've been noticed.

Desafios Adaptativos

We developed a library of desafios adaptativos that companies can customize by position and culture. They assess critical reasoning, creativity, and cultural fit far more revealingly than traditional multiple-choice questions.

Empresas Atendidas

The system was implemented at 10 companies during the period, including:

Casa&Video ABB Pirelli Honda + 6 empresas

Each implementation was customized to the company's culture and open roles, but shared the same design system and motivational principles — ensuring a consistent candidate experience regardless of company.

Stage 01 · Sign-Up & Onboarding

A first impression that invites, doesn't intimidate

The sign-up screen was redesigned as a welcome letter. The candidate chooses their "journey" — they don't fill out a form. The narrative tone replaces bureaucratic fields from the very first moment.

Drive: Epic Meaning (Octalysis) The candidate doesn't "apply for a job" — they "start their mission at [Company]."
Progressive disclosure Only 3 initial fields. Complexity is introduced gradually as engagement increases.
Stage 02 · Mission Dashboard

Progresso sempre visible and motivating

The dashboard replaces the "pending steps" list with a mission map. The candidate clearly sees where they are, what they've already accomplished, and what's coming next — eliminating the anxiety of uncertainty.

Drive: Accomplishment (Octalysis) Real-time progress bars and scoring continuously activate the achievement drive.
Radical transparency Estimated timeline, evaluation criteria, and recruiter contact visible to everyone.
2/4 conquistados
Etapa 03 · Desafio Adaptativo

Evaluation as an experience, not a test

Challenges are presented as timed missions with narrative context. The candidate solves real work situations — not abstract questions — which reveals soft skills organically.

Drive: Unpredictability (Octalysis) Every process has unique, adaptively generated challenges — with no possibility of "memorizing answers."
Real situations "How would you handle..." — assesses contextual reasoning, not memorization of correct answers.
Stage 04 · Achievements & Progression Loop

Reconhecimento que keeps the candidate invested

When completing each mission, the candidate receives immediate feedback, badges, and points. It's not decoration — it's the achievement drive in action. Every unlocked achievement increases the likelihood of completing the next stage.

Feedback positivo imediato An achievement animation celebrates every stage — 97% of candidates completed the process after earning their first badge.
Next challenge always visible The achievement screen immediately shows what comes next — the "next step" is one click away.
